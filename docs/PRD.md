## 1. Vision
A national open-source database that connects every active environmental issue in India: which projects are clearing forest, which companies are behind them, the legal position and what is missing, GHG emissions and biodiversity-loss data, the people, organisations and lawyers working on each case, status, and help needed (money and people). Anyone can see who to support. Focus: data that shows the reputational cost to companies and authorities. Every claim traces to its original source.

## 2. Users
- Volunteers and activists who want to know where to help.
- Researchers, RTI filers, lawyers and local leaders who add data.
- Journalists and the public who check claims.
- Institutes and coordinators who run campaigns and tasks.

## 3. Goals and non-goals
Goals: trustworthy sourced data; easy navigation; open for anyone to contribute.
Non-goals: none agreed yet.

## 5. Data and sources
1. Government websites (who is in charge); Parivesh portal, used with care.
2. Research papers: emission and biodiversity estimates.
3. Local people, leaders, organisations: human-rights stories.
4. Web search: companies, shareholders, products.
5. Reliable news sources.
6. RTI replies and public participation, so government data is not the only source.
Rule: all information traces back to the original source.

## 6. Open questions
- How is data verified, and who are the reviewers?
- Who owns moderation of user content?
- Tech stack, database and hosting.
