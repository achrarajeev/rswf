## 1. The core promise

See a problem near you. Find who can fix it. Check whether they did.

Behind it: every environmental issue and every NGO in one open place, with the money, the promise and the result visible, and every claim traced to its source.

## 2. Value by audience

| Audience | What they get | Why they care |
|---|---|---|
| **Individual who sees a problem** (a dumped waste site, a felled tree, a polluted lake) | Post the problem with evidence. See NGOs working on that cause nearby, what they say they do, and how funded they are. Contact the right one. Come back and check whether the response matched the promise | Today they do not know whom to call, or whether the call led anywhere |
| **Volunteer** | See open tasks on live issues, join one, appear as a contributor | Know where help is needed instead of guessing |
| **Donor or supporter** | See what an NGO received and says it spent, what it promises, and what people on the ground report | Confidence that money reaches its purpose |
| **NGO** | A free public page that is verified, a way to show real work, share records by consent, and be found by people and volunteers near it | Trust, visibility, volunteers and donors. Honest NGOs gain most |
| **Researcher, lawyer, RTI filer, local leader** | One place to find and add sourced data, legal position, evidence | Saves search time; their work reaches people who can act |
| **Journalist and public** | Check a claim and see its original source and date | Faster, safer reporting |
| **Company with real CSR work** | Its CSR projects and partner NGOs shown with sources | Credit for real work, visible to the public |

## 5. Content ideas for Instagram and other channels

Start posting when step 2.1 to 2.3 of the plan work, so visitors land on real content. Until then, post what the project is and ask for volunteers and NGOs.

1. **Problem of the week.** One real local problem with a photo, its place, the NGOs nearby and how to help.
2. **"Check an NGO in 2 minutes."** A short reel: look it up, see its registration, funding, projects and response record.
3. **Weekend spotter.** A weekend call: "Spot one problem, post it with a photo."
4. **Meet an NGO.** A verified NGO shows its work and its numbers, with its consent.
5. **Behind the numbers.** One chart from public data with its source and date.
6. **How it works.** A 30-second walk through post, find, contact, check.
7. **Volunteers wanted.** A task list people can pick from.

Posting rules: each post links to the page it talks about; no accusation; no unverified numbers; credit sources.

## 6. Proof to collect before we claim it

Do not use these lines until the number or story exists.

- Number of NGOs listed and claimed.
- Number of issues posted and reviewed.
- Number of tasks taken up and done.
- Time to find an NGO for a problem (measure in tests with real users).
- Three short stories: a problem posted, an NGO contacted, a result checked (with consent).
- Quotes from Hima's institute and from an early NGO, with permission.

## 7. Words and claims to avoid

- "Corrupt", "fraud", "scam", "laundering" about any NGO or company. We show documented gaps with sources, not verdicts.
- "Verified" for an NGO unless our review passed it. "Listed on Darpan" is not "approved".
- "Official" or any use of a government emblem or the NITI Aayog name or logo. We are not a government site.
- Any figure without a source and date.
- "Real-time" for data that is hours or days old.
- Promising a feature that is only planned.
- Names or photos of beneficiaries without consent.
