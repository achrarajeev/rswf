## 1. How this was checked

- Method: two Gemini research passes (established sites, recent launches), a Gemini sceptic review, my own web searches, and opening sites in a browser.
- Evidence tags: **Opened** (I loaded the site and read it on 2026-11-10); **Search** (a web search result describes it; I did not open it); **Gemini only** (the research model says so; unconfirmed).
- Dates for recent projects come from news and Gemini; they are not verified launch dates.
- A site that blocks scripts or loads blank for my reader is marked as such. That does not mean it is dead.

## 2. Main finding

No single site found does all of what RSWF plans: environmental issues, the NGOs on each, the CSR money, people posting local problems with evidence, and ground checks of what an NGO promised. Pieces exist:

- **Conflict databases** exist (Land Conflict Watch, EJAtlas). They document conflicts. They do not connect each one to nearby NGOs, tasks and volunteers.
- **NGO profile and rating sites** exist (Give Discover, GuideStar India, Credibility Alliance). They show registrations, financials and certifications from NGO disclosures. None found ask the people the work is meant to help.
- **CSR tools** are mostly paid software for companies and NGOs (Relific, iAmpact, Transunifyy, DhwaniRIS). The public side is the government's csr.gov.in and the new PANKHUDI portal.
- **Grievance tools** exist (CPGRAMS, Swachhata, iChangeMyCity). They send complaints to authorities. They do not point people to NGOs.

**Where RSWF is different:** (1) promise vs spend vs ground reality, checked by individuals; (2) one page per environmental issue that links the data, the NGOs, the money and the tasks; (3) routing a person with a local problem to a nearby, verified NGO; (4) right of reply and mandatory evidence.

**Where RSWF should not compete:** NGO financial profile databases (Give Discover already shows yearly income and expense for 6,000+ NGOs), general conflict research, grievance filing, CSR compliance software, and raw forest or air data.

## 8. Build, link or skip

| Area | Decision |
|---|---|
| Conflict research database | **Link** to Land Conflict Watch, EJAtlas and the India Environment Portal. Build only our action layer: who helps, tasks, volunteers |
| NGO financial profiles | **Link** to Give Discover and GuideStar India. Show Darpan registration and what we verify ourselves |
| NGO ground-reality check (promise vs spend vs result) | **Build.** No site found does this |
| Company CSR spend | **Source** csr.gov.in; show only what links to our issues and NGOs |
| CSR compliance software | **Skip** |
| Forest, air, water data | **Source** from GFW, FSI, OpenAQ and others after licence checks |
| Post a local problem with evidence | **Build** for environmental problems; **link** CPGRAMS and Swachhata for formal complaints and waste |
| Match a problem to a nearby NGO | **Build** |
| Volunteering | **Build** tasks per issue; **link** to general volunteer sites |
| Accreditation | **Show** Credibility Alliance accreditation and Give's tier as indicators, with credit |

Features this removes or shrinks in the plan: a full NGO financial database (use links), a conflict encyclopedia (link), a general grievance filer (link).

## 9. Open checks and warnings

1. Ask Land Conflict Watch, Give, GuideStar India and Credibility Alliance about data sharing or partnership before building anything near their work. A link is free; a data copy needs their terms.
2. Open by hand: Sushaasan.in, GuideStar India (needs JavaScript), India Sand Watch (find the working address), iChangeMyCity, I Paid a Bribe.
3. **Do not link openbudgetsindia.org**: on 2026-11-10 its title was a casino page. Use the CivicDataLab page.
4. The Gemini output listed a project "Hisaab" in its first draft; its own review removed it as unverifiable. It is not in this file.
5. Vendor "top CSR software" lists are marketing. No claim from them is used here without an opened source.
6. Re-run this check every six months; the field moves fast.
